Frequently Asked Questions

How does Beamit work?

Beamit uses your local WiFi network to discover nearby devices and transfer files directly between them. No internet connection or cloud upload is required.

Why can't I see other devices?

Make sure both devices are connected to the same WiFi network. Also ensure that Beamit has permission to access your local network (Settings → Beamit → Local Network).

Where are received files saved?

On iPhone and iPad, files are saved to the Beamit folder in the Files app (On My iPhone/iPad → Beamit). On Mac, files are saved to your Downloads folder.

Is there a file size limit?

No! Beamit has no file size limits. You can transfer files of any size.

Is Beamit secure?

Absolutely. Beamit uses bank-grade security:

  • All transfers are encrypted with TLS (same as online banking)
  • Each device has a unique cryptographic identity
  • Transfer tokens are cryptographically random and expire automatically
  • Every transfer requires your explicit approval
  • Your files never leave your local network

Even if someone else is on your WiFi, they cannot intercept or tamper with your transfers.

Why is the transfer slow?

Transfer speed depends on your WiFi network quality and the distance between devices. For best results, ensure both devices have a strong WiFi signal.

Can I transfer to Android devices?

Yes! Beamit works across iOS, macOS, Windows, and Android. Just install Beamit on all devices you want to share between.

Can someone on my WiFi steal my files?

No. Beamit uses end-to-end encryption and cryptographic device verification. Even if someone malicious is on your network, they cannot:

  • Read your files (they're encrypted)
  • Pretend to be another device (cryptographic identity prevents this)
  • Send you files without your approval (requires user consent)
  • Intercept transfers in progress (encrypted and verified)

Do you collect any data?

Zero. Zilch. Nada. We don't have servers. We don't collect analytics. We don't track you. We literally cannot see your files because they never leave your device. Read our Privacy Policy for details.
